## 🏢 About Mithila Series

**Mithila Series** is a culture-focused **Music & Media Company** based in **Janakpurdham, Nepal**, founded by **Sanjay Kushwaha**.

The company works across **music production, film and video production, photography, cinematography, wedding films, cultural documentation, digital media, live events, podcasts, advertising, and creative content production**.

Mithila Series aims to combine the cultural identity of Mithila with modern creative technology and cinematic production, creating content that can connect local traditions with audiences around the world.

## 🎯 Mission

> **To preserve, produce, promote, and modernize Mithila's cultural and creative heritage through music, film, photography, and digital media.**

Mithila Series focuses on creating professional content while maintaining the cultural identity, language, stories, traditions, and artistic heritage of Mithila.

---

## 🌍 Vision

> **To become a globally recognized creative media platform representing Mithila and Maithili culture through world-class production.**

The long-term vision is to connect **Mithila's artists, creators, traditions, stories, and audiences** with Nepal and the global creative industry.

A Maithili romantic and emotional music project centered around the idea of choosing home, family, village life, and simple happiness instead of leaving everything behind.

**Credits include:**
- Singer & Music — Sanjay Kushwaha

- Record / Mix / Master — Mithila Series
- Production / Post — Mithila Series

### 🎤 Kisaan

A socially focused music video inspired by real-life issues involving poverty, unemployment, foreign labor migration, broken families, farmers' struggles, and social awareness.

**Production:**
- Rap / Music — Sanjay Kushwaha
- Story / Concept / Lyrics — Mohan Mahato Koiri
- Record / Mix / Master — Mithila Series
- Featured — Sanjay Kushwaha & Puja Thakur
